04 Under the hood

Small page.
Big sky.

Ocean Drive is a single HTML page powered by Three.js and WebGL. Buildings, palms, umbrellas and traffic are drawn with instanced meshes; the sunset, the sea and the road are painted on a canvas at load time. Nothing to install, nothing to download but the page itself.

01

Instanced everything

Every palm, lamp, umbrella and building is one geometry drawn many times — hundreds of objects, a handful of draw calls.

02

Textures drawn in code

Road markings, windows, neon signs and sea sparkle are generated with the 2D canvas API. There are no image assets to load.

03

WebGL renderer

Three.js r128 with fog, hemisphere lighting and a low sun. The renderer targets 60 fps and adapts its pixel ratio on smaller screens.

04

One page, no build step

Plain JavaScript and a script tag. Open the file, or serve it from anywhere static — that is the whole deployment story.

ocean-drive.html
